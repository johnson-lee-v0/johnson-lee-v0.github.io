# Johnson Lee's portfolio

## Purpose and audience

A personal portfolio for recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and visitors interested in Johnson's analytical projects. It complements a resume; it should not reproduce one. The visitor should quickly understand who Johnson is, inspect a real project, and reach LinkedIn.

## Established truth and constraints

- Preserve the headline “I find answers in complex data.”
- Preserve the brief two-point bio, its pin and graduation emojis, and University of Waterloo degree information.
- Keep professional experience concise. Do not invent contributions, outcomes, metrics, job titles, employers, dates, or personal memories.
- The work titles and public-announcement links are the existing published content, not guesses from a desired role.
- Present independent work as side projects, not client case studies. Do not add performance-result claims to homepage summaries or expose internal evaluation/limitation notes.
- The particle portrait, refined proportions, walk-in, wave, and departure transition are signature material. This redesign does not change their implementation or timing.
- Keep Travelling, Sneakers, Gaming in that order. Use genuine Travel-Log photos and StockX sneaker images. Preserve the searchable collection and its release-year ordering.
- Gaming statistics are a dated August 17, 2026 snapshot, not a live claim.
- Contact via LinkedIn and GitHub; no email address.
- Publish only with explicit user authorization. Verify the portfolio and separate public-demo deployments before reporting that the live site has changed.

## Implementation

Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript GitHub Pages project. The homepage and five linked project pages need no framework or build. The separate Equity Research demo has its own React build; only its verified public artifact may be mounted alongside the portfolio. `tools/preview.py` serves both locally without copying or publishing files.

The established project order is Equity Research, NBA game outcomes, Max temperature modeling, University Twitter analysis, and Blackjack simulator. The NBA project retains its reference to the originating Celtics repository; the old Celtics page is not a separate homepage project.
