# Travel carousel photographs

All nine active photographs are authentic existing assets from Johnson's Travel-Log repository. They were copied unchanged from the optimized `trip_images/display/960/` files on 2026-09-20. No image was generated, retouched, resized, or recompressed for this carousel.

Source repository: <https://github.com/johnson-lee-v0/Travel-Log>. Source paths below are relative to that repository.

Each place and trip was checked against that repository's `data.js`; alt text describes the actual inspected photographs, not invented personal memories. The destination metadata is in `photos.json`. Visible Polaroid captions contain only the place and trip, without a description.

The active selection contains six photographs from Japan 2026, two from Québec City 2025, and one from the Sommet Saint-Sauveur ski-area visit in 2026. Kinkaku-ji replaces Kiyomizu-dera in the carousel. The earlier `kyoto-pagoda.webp` asset remains available for recovery but is not in the carousel; its original source was `Japan_2026/kyoto/kiyomizu2.webp` (960 × 1280).

The Odaiba photograph contains both Rainbow Bridge and the Statue of Liberty replica in a single image. The Fukuoka image is the RX-93ff nu Gundam at LaLaport Fukuoka, not the Unicorn Gundam in Tokyo. Its source happens to be grouped in the `kitakyushu` folder; the stop name, summary, and photograph identify the Fukuoka attraction.
