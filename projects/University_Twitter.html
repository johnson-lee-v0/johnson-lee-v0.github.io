University Twitter analysis

What do Waterloo, U of T, and Western talk about online? An exploration of the language, sentiment, and subjects in their public posts.

R · Natural language processing · Three Ontario universities

A closer look at the language

The starting point was historical posts from the three universities. Preparing the text in R made it possible to compare common terms, sentiment, and named entities across the accounts.

The term counts show shared vocabulary; reading the posts adds context. Alumni accomplishments and new initiatives appear in the positive examples, while crises and tragedies appear in the negative examples. Those differences informed content ideas around student life, alumni, and the local community.
