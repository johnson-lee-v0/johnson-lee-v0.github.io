University Content Strategy Benchmark

A comparative NLP analysis of how Waterloo, U of T, and Western communicate with their public audiences—and what content hypotheses emerge for prospective students.

  • ~9,600 posts
  • 3 universities
  • R
  • NLP

Question

Where does each university's public content differ?

Compare themes, tone, and named entities across historical posts, then identify content gaps worth exploring.

Approach

Benchmark first, interpret second

Historical posts were prepared and analyzed in R using sentiment and entity analysis, then compared across the three institutions.

Output

Patterns translated into hypotheses

The analysis surfaced differences in student-life, alumni, and community content that could inform future content testing.
